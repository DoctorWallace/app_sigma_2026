Proposal evaluation procedure

Permanent open call · Scientific committee · Selection criteria · Deadlines

Open call

There will be a permanent open call for submission of proposals. The evaluation of the received proposals will be performed by a scientific committee formed by the following experienced scientists:

  • Dr. Nathalie Valle — LIST, Luxemburg
  • Dr. Arturs Zarins — University of Latvia, Latvia
  • Dr. Fernando Sánchez Sanz — CIEMAT, Madrid
  • Dra. Raquel González Arrabal — IFN, ETSI Industriales – UPM, Madrid
  • Dr. María González Viada — CIEMAT, Madrid
  • Dr. José Ygnacio Pastor Caño — ETSI Caminos – UPM, Madrid
  • Dr. Alejandro Moroño — CIEMAT, Madrid

Submission email

Each research proposal can be sent via the SIGMA ICTS application or by email to:

rtt_dtf@ciemat.es

Include contact details, facility requested, scope, expected outputs and any scheduling constraints.

To submit through SIGMA ICTS: register, create a proposal, complete the form, and click "Send".

Selection criteria

  1. Feasibility of the experiment (yes/no). In the affirmative case, then…
  2. Scientific quality of the proposal (rank: weak: 0 – outstanding: 5)
  3. Need for the use of this advanced infrastructure (rank: weak: 0 – outstanding: 5)
  4. Potentiality for industrial interest (rank: weak: 0 – outstanding: 5)

Proposals with a total ranking < 7 will be rejected. Rejection will be accompanied by a short report outlining the reasons.

Notifications & next steps

Accepted proposals will be notified to the submitter within 10 working days of reception. The leading requester is then invited to communicate details to the local contact.

The procedure continues with the completion of the analysis and the delivery of a report. The maximum time for each stage depends on the laboratory involved and is listed below.

Deadlines by facility

Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) related studies < 10 days < 3 months
